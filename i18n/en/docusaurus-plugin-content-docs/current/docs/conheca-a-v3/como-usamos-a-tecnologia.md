#  How we use technology

##  Technological innovation at V3

V3 stands out for its focus on implementing modern and innovative technologies. We are constantly exploring and developing solutions that leverage state-of-the-art engineering in software, data processing, and especially artificial intelligence and computer vision.

##  Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision

###  Artificial Intelligence (AI)
Artificial intelligence represents computational systems capable of performing tasks that would normally require human intelligence. At V3, we use advanced machine learning and deep learning algorithms to analyze large volumes of data, identify complex patterns, and generate valuable insights that would be impossible to obtain through traditional methods.

###  Computer Vision
Computer vision is a field of AI that enables computers to "see" and interpret the visual world like humans do. Through sophisticated image processing techniques and convolutional neural networks, our systems can detect objects, recognize visual patterns, and understand complex scenes in real time.

One of V3's differentiators is its **Computer Vision** processing engine, which can be explored in more detail in the [Vision section](/docs/docs/integracao-e-operacao/api/visoes).

##  Our technological pillars

Our technological approach is based on two fundamental concepts:

### 1.  Dashcams for real-world capture

Currently, our main focus is on using high-quality, low-cost dashcams (dashboard cameras) installed in light vehicles. These cameras:

-  **Continuously record the route**: Capture the entire vehicle journey, generating a complete visual record of the trip
-  **Work in various conditions**: Adapt to different light levels, from intense sunlight to nighttime conditions
-  **Have a wide field of view**: Ensure comprehensive coverage of the road and surrounding environment
-  **Can integrate with the vehicle**: Can collect additional data via the CAN (Controller Area Network) protocol, providing access to information such as speed, RPM, acceleration, braking, seat belt status, and other real-time vehicle data

This camera-focused approach allows us to collect extremely rich visual datasets about traffic conditions, driving behavior, and road infrastructure status — all from vehicles operating normally in day-to-day use.

### 2.  AI models for interpretation and insights

We use, develop, and implement artificial intelligence models that enable the creation of solutions that:

-  Process and analyze raw data captured by sensors
-  Identify meaningful patterns amid noise and irrelevant information
-  Infer real-world events and conditions
-  Generate actionable insights that enable more efficient decision-making
-  Learn continuously, improving with each new interaction and dataset

##  Practical applications in mobility

As specialists in computer vision for mobility, we research and develop solutions that transform how vehicles and infrastructure interact. Our solution is constantly evolving, and we are designing it to support various scenarios. Some practical examples include:

###  Safety
-  **Drowsiness detection**: Continuous monitoring of the driver's facial expressions and eye patterns to identify signs of fatigue or loss of attention
-  **PPE usage verification**: Automatic identification of correct use of personal protective equipment, such as seat belts and helmets
-  **Cell phone usage detection**: Identification of distracting behaviors, such as mobile device use while driving

###  Infrastructure monitoring
-  **Pothole and road surface defect detection**: Proactive identification of road surface issues that may compromise safety
-  **Road condition analysis**: Monitoring conditions such as water on the road, debris, or landslides that pose risks
-  **Sign recognition and interpretation**: Automatic reading of signs, traffic lights, and road markings

###  Operational optimization
-  **Driving behavior analysis**: Identification of acceleration, braking, and maneuver patterns to improve efficiency and safety
-  **Preventive maintenance prediction**: Early detection of mechanical issues based on visual and acoustic patterns
-  **Real-time route optimization**: Route adjustments based on visually detected conditions

These applications represent only a fraction of the transformative potential of computer vision and AI in the mobility field — areas where V3 continues to innovate and set new standards of technological excellence.
