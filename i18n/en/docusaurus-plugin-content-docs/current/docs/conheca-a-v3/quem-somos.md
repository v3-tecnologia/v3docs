#  Who we are

##  Introduction to V3

Welcome to the first step of our journey to learn about V3. In this opening chapter, we introduce our company, technologies, and target market in the video telematics space.

##  Our History and Scope

Founded in 2018, V3 is at the forefront of the digital transformation of intelligent mobility. Our mission is to revolutionize fleet management through advanced video telematics solutions, combining computer vision and artificial intelligence to create a complete and integrated ecosystem.

###  Our Identity

The name **V3** represents the three fundamental pillars of our technology:
- **Vehicle**: Focus on mobility and fleet management
- **Video**: Image capture and processing technology
- **Vision**: Artificial intelligence and advanced analytics

###  Our Solution

We offer a **complete** ecosystem that covers:
- **Hardware**: Robust and optimized devices
- **Firmware**: High-performance embedded software
- **Storage**: Secure and scalable infrastructure
- **Visualization**: Intuitive web interface
- **Integration**: Robust and well-documented APIs

###  Our Business Model

We operate in a B2B model, providing technology so our partners can:
- Offer complete telematics solutions
- Implement advanced video telematics
- Meet the specific needs of fleet operators
- Create competitive differentiators in the market
