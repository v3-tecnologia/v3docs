#  Mission, Vision, and Values

##  Our Mission

:::info[Mission]

Our mission is to develop consistent innovations, delivering cutting-edge technology for fleet management that is always productive, human, and safe.

:::

Our mission reflects our commitment to creating and providing technological solutions that not only solve real problems, but also open new paths for the evolution of the mobility sector.

##  Our Vision

:::tip[Vision]

To establish ourselves as a global reference in the integration of computer vision technologies with a focus on mobility, transforming how vehicles interact with their environment and providing greater safety and efficiency.

:::

We envision a future where our technology is an essential component in intelligent mobility solutions around the world, creating a safer and more efficient ecosystem for everyone.

##  Our Values

Our values are the pillars that guide our decisions and daily behaviors:

###  **Respect Always**
We cultivate an environment where mutual respect is fundamental, building relationships based on unity and trust among colleagues, partners, and customers.

###  **Welcoming Environment**
We create a space where each person can develop their full potential, respecting individuality and encouraging personal and professional growth — "your dreams, your track."

###  **Creative Attitudes**
We value people who take initiative, take a stand, and act creatively in the face of challenges, contributing innovative solutions and diverse perspectives.

###  **Simplicity in Relationships**
We prioritize authenticity and transparency in all interactions, eliminating unnecessary complexity to build genuine and effective relationships.

###  **Purposeful Work**
We seek consistency and ownership in every project, ensuring our actions are aligned with greater objectives and generate positive impact.

##  Our Principles

The principles that guide our daily operations:

1.  **Respect builds trust** — The foundation for lasting and productive relationships at all levels.

2.  **Love what you do** — Passion and dedication as fuel for excellence.

3.  **Always work as a team** — We value collaboration over individual competition.

4.  **A good team has complementary profiles** — Diversity of skills and perspectives as a creative force.

5.  **The problem is ours, the solution is too** — We take responsibility and ownership in solving challenges.

6.  **Stop to: reflect, propose, and commit** — We value strategic thinking before action.

7.  **Nothing replaces a present face-to-face** — We prioritize direct and personal communication whenever possible.

8.  **Preparation + Anticipation ⇒ Good execution** — Careful planning as the basis for exceptional results.

9.  **A good idea only counts if implemented** — We value execution and materialization of ideas.

10.  **Innovation only counts if sustainable** — We pursue innovations that generate lasting and responsible value.

##  What do we encourage?

Our work environment and culture actively promote:

###  **Using the right tool for each challenge**
We choose technologies and methodologies based on their suitability to the problem, not passing trends.

###  **Minimum viable product first**
We prioritize incremental deliveries that generate value quickly, enabling continuous validation and learning.

###  **User-centered design**
We develop our solutions focused on the real needs of users, ensuring intuitive and effective experiences.

###  **Assertive communication**
We value clear, direct, and respectful messages that promote understanding and efficient collaboration.
