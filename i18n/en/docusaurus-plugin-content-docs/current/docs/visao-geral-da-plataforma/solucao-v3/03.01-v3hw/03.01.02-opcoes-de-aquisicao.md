# Acquisition Options

V3 offers different options for acquiring the hardware devices required to implement our solution in your fleet. We understand that each company has distinct needs and capabilities, which is why we developed a flexible business model.

We offer flexibility in acquiring compatible devices, allowing you to choose the option best suited to your business model:

1. **Direct purchase from V3**: Acquire certified hardware directly from us, with guaranteed compatibility and streamlined technical support.

2. **Partner network**: Use our network of certified partners who can provide devices ready for integration.

3. **Direct purchase from manufacturers**: For customers who prefer to negotiate directly with manufacturers, we provide the specifications required to ensure compatibility.

4. **Leveraging existing devices**: If your company already has devices installed in your fleet that are among the compatible models, you can incorporate them into the V3 ecosystem without the need for replacement.
