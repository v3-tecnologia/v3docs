# Compatible Models

Our solution was developed to work with a variety of devices available on the market. Compatible models undergo rigorous certification testing to ensure optimal performance with our platform.

In the following sections, you will learn in detail about each model compatible with V3, their technical specifications, features, and recommended use cases, allowing you to choose the equipment best suited to your specific needs.

## JC400P - Jimi IoT

The **JC400P** from Jimi IoT is a professional dashcam that combines high-quality recording with advanced connectivity.

<p align="center">
  <img src="/img/docs/solucao-v3/devices/jc400p/1.png" alt="JC400P - Front view" width="60%" />
</p>

### Technical Specifications

- **Processor:** Quad-core 1.4GHz A53
- **Operating System:** Android 8.1 GO
- **Front Camera:** 1920×1080/25FPS/F2.2/full color
- **Rear Camera:** 1280×720/15FPS/F2.5/full color (daytime) & monochrome (nighttime)
- **Display:** 4-inch touch-sensitive IPS
- **Video Format:** H.264

#### Connectivity
- **WiFi:** 2.4GHz, 802.11 b/g/n
- **Bluetooth:** BLE 4.0
- **GNSS:** GPS+BDS
- **Mobile Network - Eurasian Version:**
  - FDD LTE: B1/B3/B5/B7/B8/B19/B20
  - TDD LTE: B38/B39/B40/B41 (100M)
  - WCDMA: B1/B2/B5/B8
  - GSM: 850/900/1800/1900MHz
- **Mobile Network - American Version:**
  - FDD LTE: B2/B3/B4/B5/B7/B12/B17
  - TDD LTE: B38/B41 (100M)
  - WCDMA: B2/B4/B5
  - GSM: 850/1800/1900MHz

#### Storage and Interfaces
- **Storage:** TF Card (Max. 256GB)
- **SIM Card:** Nano
- **Data Interface:** Micro USB 2.0
- **Indicator LEDs:** Red (Power), Blue (Cellular), Green (GNSS)

#### Sensors and Additional Hardware
- **Sensors:** 6-axis
- **Microphone:** Integrated
- **Speaker:** Integrated

#### Physical Specifications
- **Battery:** External 450mAh
- **Power:** Power cable: B+/ACC/GND
- **Operating Temperature:** -20°C to +65°C
- **Operating Voltage:** 9-30V DC
- **Weight:** 233g
- **Dimensions:** 109x69x52mm
- **Certifications:** FCC/CE/RoHS
