# Introduction

## Overview

V3 offers a modular solution that integrates to form a complete video telematics ecosystem. Each module covers a specific stage of our process, from data capture to visualization and integration.

## Modular Architecture

![Solução V3](/img/docs/solucao-v3/solucao-v3.png)

Our architecture consists of three main modules:

- **V3HW**: Responsible for acquiring the equipment required for data capture. This equipment can be purchased directly from V3, through direct contact with manufacturers, or via strategic partnerships.

- **V3CORE**: Central platform composed of two essential submodules:
  - **V3FW**: Our embedded software solution installed on physical devices.
  - **V3CLOUD**: Our cloud technology solution responsible for communicating with devices through the firmware, preparing, processing, and storing data to make it available to the V3View layer.

- **V3VIEW**: Information delivery layer, accessible in three different ways:
  - **V3API**: Set of APIs documented in this portal that allow you to obtain processed and organized information generated by the devices.
  - **V3WEB**: White-label desktop web application that offers basic functionality without the need to consume, process, and display data. Ideal solution for companies without their own development team, including early-stage businesses. Allows appearance customization according to your company's or your clients' visual identity.
  - **V3APP**: Application for Android and iOS devices that offers the same features available on the desktop web platform.
