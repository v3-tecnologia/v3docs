# Driver Behavior

The driver behavior monitoring system uses accelerometer data to identify driving patterns and detect events that may represent safety risks.

## Accelerometer

An **accelerometer** is an electromechanical device that measures acceleration forces, whether static (such as gravity) or dynamic (resulting from movement and vibrations). In the context of V3FW, the accelerometer allows identification of changes in driver behavior and detection of significant events.

### Measurement Axes

Accelerometer measurements are oriented along three axes:

- **Longitudinal (X)**: Detects forward and backward movement (acceleration and braking)
- **Lateral (Y)**: Detects lateral movement (turns)
- **Vertical (Z)**: Detects upward and downward movement (rollovers)

## Mapped Events

The system is capable of identifying various driver behavior patterns:

| **Event** | **Description** | **Primary Axis** |
|------------|---------------|---------------------|
| **Harsh Acceleration** | Rapid acceleration in a straight line | Longitudinal (X) |
| **Harsh Braking** | Rapid deceleration in a straight line | Longitudinal (X) |
| **Improper Turn** | Dangerous left or right turns | Lateral (Y) |
| **Suspected Impact** | Any detected impact | Multiple axes |

## Detection System

To accurately identify these events, the system uses a combination of:

- **GPS Data**: Determines exact location and vehicle speed
- **Accelerometer Data**: Provides precise acceleration measurements across three axes
- **Processing Algorithms**: Analyze readings and apply filters to reduce false positives

## Monitoring Benefits

Driver behavior monitoring offers multiple advantages:

- **Improved Safety**: Identification of drivers with risky driving patterns
- **Cost Reduction**: Less vehicle wear due to smoother driving
- **Fuel Savings**: More efficient driving results in lower consumption
- **Targeted Training**: Ability to offer specific training based on collected data
- **Accident Prevention**: Real-time alerts for risky behaviors
