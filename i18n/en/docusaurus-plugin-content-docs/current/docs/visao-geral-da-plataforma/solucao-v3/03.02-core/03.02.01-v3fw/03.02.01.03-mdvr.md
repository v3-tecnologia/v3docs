# MDVR (Mobile Digital Video Recorder)

**MDVR** is a **V3FW** component responsible for real-time recording from cameras available on devices, enabling local storage and remote video retrieval.

## How It Works

The system works with continuous recording from cameras available on devices. The main characteristics are:

- **Local Storage**: Videos are stored directly on the device, limited by SD card capacity.
- **Cyclic Recording**: When storage is full, the system automatically replaces the oldest recordings with the most recent ones.
- **Selective Upload**: The system uploads to the cloud only at strategic moments during the trip.

## Strategic Upload Moments

The system prioritizes video uploads in specific situations:

- **Ignition Events**: When the ignition is turned on or off
- **Risk Behaviors**: Detection of harsh acceleration, harsh braking, or dangerous maneuvers
- **DMS Alerts**: When the system detects drowsiness, distraction, or other risky driver behaviors
- **Facial Recognition**: During driver authentication

## Benefits

MDVR offers several advantages for fleet management:

- **Visual Evidence**: Provides visual records that can be used for incident analysis
- **Remote Retrieval**: Allows access to specific recordings (if still available) without requiring physical access to the vehicle
- **Bandwidth Optimization**: By uploading only at strategic moments, it saves bandwidth and transmission costs
- **Complete History**: Maintains a continuous record of vehicle operations in local storage

## Accessing Recordings

Videos uploaded to the cloud are available (initially for up to 3 months) through the **V3CLOUD** platform, enabling historical queries and remote viewing of important events.
