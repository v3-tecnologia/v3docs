# JC400P

## Welcome

Welcome to the V3HW Installation Manual. This document aims to ensure the installation process is carried out correctly, safely, and efficiently.

 **Attention:** Installation that does not follow the standards may compromise data quality, directly impact service delivery, and cause losses for your company and your customers.

Follow this manual rigorously to ensure service quality and the best performance of the technology.

## Installation Step by Step

---

### 1. Identifying Tap Points

 **Never rely on wire color alone. Always use a multimeter to confirm!**

:::danger[IMPORTANT]

Properly insulate all taps to prevent short circuits!

:::

---

#### **T30 – Direct Positive**

- Locate a point in the fuse box that is direct battery power.
- Verify with a multimeter:
  - Voltage must be present **constantly**, even with the ignition off.
  - Wait a few minutes after turning off the ignition to confirm it is **not a timed circuit**.
-  An incorrect tap point may cause operational failures or data loss.

---

#### **T15 – Ignition-Switched Power**

- Find a point in the fuse box that:
  - Shows **0V with the ignition off**.
  - Shows **12V or 24V (depending on the vehicle) with the ignition on**.
- Verify there is no influence from accessories, open doors, or timers.

---

#### **GND – Ground**

- Use metallic points on the vehicle structure (chassis).
- Verify continuity with a multimeter.
-  **Never use already grounded wires.** This prevents issues such as floating ground, which can cause equipment damage and electrical problems.

---

### 2. Wire Connection

- Connect the T30, T15, and GND cables.
- Use electrical tape to individually cover stripped wires.
- Organize harnesses with cable ties to prevent poor contact or short circuits.

---

### 3. Backup Battery Installation

- Position the battery near the power tap points.
- Secure it in a safe, protected location, away from heat exposure (do not install on the ceiling or upper dashboard).
- Use double-sided tape after cleaning the surface with isopropyl alcohol.
- If possible, ensure secure mounting with plastic straps (i.e., nylon cable ties)

---

### 4. Harness Routing

- Route the power and battery cables to the windshield area (near the rearview mirror).
- Use the A-pillar or headliner trim.
-  Pay extra attention to vehicles with **AirBag in the A-pillar**.
- The harness must not:
  - Block latches.
  - Be exposed.
  - Cross with antennas (to avoid interference).

---

### 5. Device Positioning

- The **V3HW** must be installed on the **vehicle windshield**, preferably near the center of the windshield, just below the rearview mirror. However, pay attention to the rearview mirror and sun visor — these must not obstruct the internal camera lens view.
- Choose a surface that is:
  - Smooth and clean (use 70% isopropyl alcohol).
  - Away from direct heat or water exposure.
  - Distant from electronic modules.
  - Suitable for double-sided tape adhesion
- Attach the base to the camera
  - Slide until fully seated
  - Mount the base on the windshield:
    - Press for 15 seconds
    - Remove the device from the base and press again for another 15 seconds
    - Reattach the device to the base
- The V3HW must be installed horizontally, keeping the V3 logo upright
- Adjust the external camera to capture the full road view, similar to what the driver sees
  - Heavy vehicles require different adjustment than light vehicles
- **Tip:** When viewed from the side of the V3HW, the external camera should point toward the horizon (or slightly downward). It should be able to view the road and part of the hood (in the case of a light vehicle).
- **Remove the protective film from the external camera**

### 6. Final Connection and Boot Process

- Connect the power harness to the V3HW.
- The **red LED lights solid**, indicating correct power supply.
- Turn on the ignition (T15 ignition-switched power):
  - Confirm the blue LED blinks and the red LED remains lit.
- After 15 seconds, turn off the ignition:
  - The red LED should remain lit (powered by T30 and backup battery).
- Connect the backup battery harness.

:::warning[IMPORTANT]

The first boot process occurs only after ignition-switched power is applied! Additionally, during the first boot, the device will load the entire OS, and it should take 1 to 3 minutes to start and launch the application (potentially up to 5 minutes in some cases).

:::

:::warning[IMPORTANT]

The blue LED indicates internet connectivity. If the device has no network (or no WiFi), this LED will not blink. An alternative way to verify that the V3HW is enabled is by "listening" for the welcome / goodbye message when toggling the vehicle ignition (ignition-switched power).

:::

## 7. Platform Validation

1. Access [https://web.v3.com.br](https://web.v3.com.br)
2. Log in.
3. Locate the installed vehicle.
4. Start the vehicle and verify:
   - **Solid red LED**
   - **Blinking blue LED**
   - Ignition event recorded correctly.
   - Coherent GPS location.
   - Internal and external image recording.

:::warning[IMPORTANT]

For installations in underground areas or regions that may cause positioning distortion (dispersion), location may fluctuate or not reflect reality. You can verify if this is the case by driving the vehicle through an open area for a few minutes, so it generates new positions and updates the GPS fix.

:::

## Final Recommendations

- We recommend applying **security seal labels** on the connection between the harness and the device.
- Perform a vehicle checklist:
  - Verify everything works normally after installation.
- Finish wire routing:
  - Use electrical tape and cable ties to ensure organization and safety.

We suggest a pre- and post-installation checklist. This provides documentation to address possible vehicle failures caused by the "installer" during the process described here.
